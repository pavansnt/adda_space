About the Initiative

Where communities meet, share, and act.

Adda Space is an open, voluntary, community-driven initiative that activates physical neighborhood spaces across cities for dialogues, skill sharing, and civic action.

The Spirit of the Adda

In Indian culture, an adda has always been an informal gathering—a tea stall bench, a veranda, or a neighborhood corner where people pause their daily routines to debate ideas, share stories, and explore questions of community and culture.

Adda Space reimagines this tradition for modern cities. We partner with public libraries, community halls, civic centers, and grassroots spaces to host open sessions where residents can come together as active participants rather than passive spectators.

Core Principles

Always Free & Open No entry fees, no commercial monetization, and no paywalls. Adda Space is committed to public commons principles.
Voluntary & Collaborative Gatherings are organised by and for the community. Anyone with a thoughtful idea or skill to share can step forward as a host.
Hyper-Local & Rooted Each session addresses local concerns—from urban trees and water bodies to digital tools and community crafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy a ticket?

No. All Adda Space gatherings are completely free of charge. We do not operate ticket sales, paid registrations, or commercial checkout systems.

Can I bring a friend or child?

Yes. Gatherings are open to all community members unless a specific session notes age guidelines (e.g. youth-focused maker jams). Check the individual gathering description for details.

Who supports this initiative?

Adda Space is made possible through the collaboration of local community volunteers, grassroots civic leaders, and Reap Benefit.
