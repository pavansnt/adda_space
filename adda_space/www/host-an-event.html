Community Hosting

Host a gathering at Adda Space.

Have an idea for a discussion, civic workshop, storytelling session, or hands-on jam? We help community members activate neighbourhood spaces in their cities.

What Gatherings Fit Adda Space?

Adda Space is designed for participatory, community-led formats rather than conventional lectures. We encourage sessions that foster real interaction:

Civic & Neighbourhood Jams Community mapping, waste audits, local public space audits, and neighbourhood improvement discussions.
Ecology & Climate Circles Conversations on urban trees, water harvesting, local biodiversity, and citizen science observation.
Learning & Skill Shares Hands-on workshops on open-source tools, data literacy, composting, repair cafes, and zine making.
Arts, Books & Dialogue Reading circles, poetry exchanges, film screenings with reflection, and intergenerational oral history sessions.

Hosting Guidelines

  • Free Admission: Events must remain completely free and accessible. No cover charges, registration fees, or mandatory product purchases.
  • Non-Commercial: Adda Space cannot be used for direct commercial marketing, brand promotions, or sales pitches.
  • Inclusive & Respectful: Sessions must be welcoming to diverse participants regardless of age, background, identity, or prior expertise.
  • Caring for the Commons: Hosts are responsible for leaving community spaces clean and respecting neighbourhood quiet hours.

How to Propose a Gathering

The team reviews event proposals on a rolling basis. Once submitted, our volunteer coordinators will connect with you to confirm venue availability in your city and list the approved gathering on this public calendar.
